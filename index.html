Welcome to Cat's Blood!

Cat's Blood is a turn-based card game based of Queen's Blood in Final Fantasy VII: Rebirth.

How to play!

All cards have a Value, unique abilities which can either aid you or harm your opponent and a slot layout cost and display that shows which slots are on your side.

Each player starts with 5 cards and you can swap out a card if you'd like. You then take turns to place a card on the field until no more slots are left, then add up all the points in a row. Whoever has the most wins those points.

Winning a row means you win the total points in that row of your cards.
